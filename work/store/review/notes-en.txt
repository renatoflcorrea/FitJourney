WHAT THE APP DOES
FitJourney plans and logs gym workouts and a diet plan, on iPhone and Apple Watch. No sign-in or account is required; all data is stored on the device.

Setup: Today tab > profile button (top right) > Coach & AI > Provider: OpenCode Go > paste the key > Save. The model list loads from the account; the default model (gpt-6-luna) reads images.

HOW TO REVIEW THE AI FEATURES (sample content is built into the app)
1. Training tab > + > Import with AI > "Try with a sample". This adds a fictional workout sheet image bundled in the app ("Add the other 3 sample sheets" adds a full 4-routine program). Tap Analyze.
2. Before anything is sent, a "Before sending" screen states exactly what will be sent, names who receives it (OpenCode, and the company that created the selected model) with links to their privacy policies and to FitJourney's privacy policy, and asks for permission: Allow / Don't Allow. Don't Allow sends nothing and keeps the app fully usable without AI.
3. After Allow, the app reads the sheet, asks only what is unclear and shows the routines for review before creating the program.
4. Diet tab > Import with AI > "Try with a sample" imports a bundled 7-page sample meal plan (PDF). Diet tab > log a meal > "Try with a sample" fills a sample meal description.
5. Coach tab: the same consent, naming the recipient, with optional data categories (Profile, Workouts, Diet, Health), all off by default.
6. Settings > Coach & AI > "What's shared and with whom" shows what is allowed and for whom, and lets the user turn off any feature or category or revoke everything. Switching provider or model creator asks for permission again.

DATA SHARING (Guidelines 5.1.1(i) and 5.1.2(i))
AI requests go directly from the device to the provider the user configured; there is no FitJourney server and the developer receives nothing. Permission is asked per feature and per recipient before the first request. The privacy policy (https://renatocorrea.com.br/FitJourney/en/privacy/#ai) lists what is sent for each feature, how it is used, every possible recipient with its privacy policy, and the equal-protection statement.

CHINA (Guideline 5)
The app is no longer distributed in China mainland (removed in Availability).

OTHER FEATURES
- Today tab: "Start with an example (ABC)" creates a sample program. Start workout, complete sets with one tap; the rest timer runs in the Live Activity / Dynamic Island.
- Apple Watch: a workout started on iPhone can be controlled on the watch (sets, load with the Digital Crown, rest, pause, finish) with live heart rate.
- Health tab: with permission, reads heart rate, resting heart rate, workouts, active energy, steps and body mass, and writes workouts to Apple Health.

The app works fully without AI, without an Apple Watch and without network access (except external exercise videos and AI requests).
