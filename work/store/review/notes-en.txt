WHAT THE APP DOES
FitJourney plans and logs gym workouts and a diet plan, on iPhone and Apple Watch. No sign-in or account is required; all data stays on the device. Every AI feature is optional.

SAMPLE CONTENT (built into the app, no files needed)
1. Workouts tab > + > Import with AI > PDF or file > "Try with a sample" adds a fictional workout sheet image ("Add the other 3 sample sheets" adds a 4-routine program). Tap Analyze.
2. Diet tab > ... > Import with AI > "Try with a sample" adds a 7-page sample meal plan (PDF).
3. Diet tab > "What did you eat?" > "Try with a sample" fills a sample meal description.

PERMISSION BEFORE SENDING (Guidelines 5.1.1(i) and 5.1.2(i))
- Before the first request of each feature, a sheet (e.g. "Send the workout sheet to OpenCode?") lists what is sent and what is not, names who receives it (the provider and, for routers like OpenCode or OpenRouter, the company that created the selected model), links to their privacy policies and to FitJourney's, and asks Allow / Don't Allow. Don't Allow sends nothing; the app stays fully usable.
- Permission is per feature (Import, Meal log, Coach) and per recipient. Changing provider or model creator asks again.
- Coach: data categories (Profile, Workouts, Diet, Health) are optional and off by default.
- Settings > Coach & AI > "What's shared and with whom" shows what is allowed and for whom, and turns off any feature or category, or revokes everything.
- Requests go straight from the device to the configured provider. There is no FitJourney server; the developer receives nothing.
- Privacy policy, AI section (what is sent per feature, how it is used, every recipient and its policy): https://renatocorrea.com.br/FitJourney/en/privacy/#ai

DAILY DIET GOALS
When an imported plan lists no daily goals, the review shows "Estimate goals with AI". It sends only the list of meals, foods and amounts (no files) to the same provider already allowed, and shows the result marked "Estimated by AI" with Remove. If the foods already list calories and macros, the app adds them up on the device, without AI. In the plan editor, "Add up from the plan's foods" and "Estimate with Coach" (a proposal you approve) fill missing goals.

MODELS
The model picker lists the current models from the provider account (refreshed every 24 h, with a manual refresh), with the newest small model as default.

CHINA (Guideline 5)
China mainland was removed in Pricing and Availability. The app is no longer distributed there.

OTHER FEATURES
- Today tab: "Start with an example (ABC)" creates a sample program. Start a workout, complete sets with one tap; the rest timer runs in the Live Activity / Dynamic Island.
- Apple Watch: a workout started on iPhone can be controlled on the watch (sets, load with the Digital Crown, rest, pause, finish) with live heart rate.
- Health tab: with permission, reads heart rate, resting heart rate, workouts, active energy, steps and body mass, and writes workouts to Apple Health.

The app works fully without AI, without an Apple Watch and offline (except AI requests and external exercise videos).
