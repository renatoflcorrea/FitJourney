Guideline 2.1(a) - Demo access
- A demo API key (OpenCode Go) and setup steps are in the App Review Information notes.
- The app now includes sample content, so every AI feature can be tested without your own files: "Try with a sample" in Import with AI adds a fictional workout sheet image (Workouts) or a 7-page sample meal plan (Diet), and the meal log has a sample description.

Guidelines 5.1.1(i) and 5.1.2(i) - Data shared with third-party AI
- Before any data is sent, the app shows a permission sheet that explains what will be sent for that feature and what will not, names who receives it (the configured provider and, for routers like OpenCode or OpenRouter, also the company that created the model), links to their privacy policies, and asks Allow / Don't Allow.
- Permission is per feature and per recipient; changing provider or model creator asks again. Don't Allow sends nothing and the app remains fully usable.
- Settings > Coach & AI > "What's shared and with whom" shows what is allowed and lets the user turn off any feature or data category, or revoke everything.
- Requests go directly from the device to the provider the user chose. We run no server and receive no user data.
- The privacy policy has a dedicated AI section listing what is sent for each feature, how it is used, every recipient with its privacy policy, and the equal-protection statement: https://renatocorrea.com.br/FitJourney/en/privacy/#ai

Guideline 5 - China mainland
- We removed China mainland in Pricing and Availability. The app is no longer distributed there.
