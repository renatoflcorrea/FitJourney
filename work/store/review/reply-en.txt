Thank you for the detailed feedback. Build {{BUILD}} addresses each point:

Guideline 2.1(a) - Demo access
- A demo API key (OpenCode Go) and setup steps are now in the App Review Information notes.
- The app now includes sample content: in Import with AI, "Try with a sample" adds a fictional workout sheet image bundled in the app (and a 7-page sample meal plan in the Diet tab), so every AI feature can be tested without your own files.

Guidelines 5.1.1(i) and 5.1.2(i) - Data shared with third-party AI
- Before any data is sent, the app shows a "Before sending" screen that explains exactly what will be sent for that feature, names who receives it (the configured provider, and for routers like OpenCode or OpenRouter also the company that created the model) with links to their privacy policies, and asks for permission with Allow / Don't Allow.
- Permission is per feature and per recipient. Switching providers or model creators asks again. Don't Allow sends nothing and the app remains fully usable.
- Settings > Coach & AI > "What's shared and with whom" shows what is allowed and lets the user turn off any feature or data category, or revoke everything.
- The privacy policy now has a dedicated AI section listing what is collected for each feature, how it is used, every recipient with its privacy policy, and the equal-protection statement: https://renatocorrea.com.br/FitJourney/en/privacy/#ai

Guideline 5 - China
- The app is no longer distributed in China mainland; we removed that storefront in Availability.

The App Review Information notes describe the steps to review these features.
